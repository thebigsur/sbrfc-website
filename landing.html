Why play?

  • Never played? Perfect.

    Every team teaches beginners from day one. No gear or experience required.

  • Fun and social.

    Make friends, get fit outdoors on the coast, and join a team that has your back.

  • Every body, every level.

    Rugby needs every shape, size and speed.

How to get started

  1. Pick your team above.

  2. Visit the club's site for practice times and how to join.

  3. Show up in athletic clothes. The coaches handle the rest.

Common questions

Do I need experience?
No.
Is it safe?
Coaches teach proper technique, and youth play is age-appropriate.
What should I bring?
Athletic clothes, water, boots if you have them.
How old do I need to be?
Youth U8–U18, adults 18+.
